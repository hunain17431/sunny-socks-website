Privacy & Policy

Introduction

Last updated: 29/10/2026

At Sunny Socks, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and what rights you have.

Information we collect

We collect the details you give us when you place an order or send an inquiry, such as your name, email address, delivery address and phone number. We also collect basic technical data, like your browser type and the pages you visit on our website.

How we use your information

We use your information to process and deliver your orders, answer your questions and send order updates. With your consent, we may also send you news about new collections, which you can unsubscribe from at any time.

Legal basis for processing

We only process your data when we have a lawful reason to do so under the GDPR. This includes fulfilling a contract with you, meeting our legal obligations, pursuing our legitimate interests or acting on your consent.

Sharing your information

We share your data only with trusted partners who help us run our business, such as payment providers, delivery companies and email services. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

Cookies and tracking

Our website uses cookies to keep it working properly and to understand how visitors use it. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies, and you can change your browser settings at any time to block them.

Data retention

We keep your personal data only for as long as we need it for the purposes described here. Order records are kept for as long as required by Dutch tax and accounting rules, and other data is deleted once it is no longer needed.

Data security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. Payments are handled by secure, certified payment providers.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to or request a copy of it. You can also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) if you are unhappy with how we handle your data.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or want to exercise your rights, please contact us at Sunny Socks, Oudegracht 245, 3511 NJ Utrecht, The Netherlands, or use the contact page on our website.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, and the latest version will always be published on this page. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
