Sustainability

Our Materials

Our socks are made from organic cotton, recycled yarns and responsibly sourced wool. We choose soft, durable fibres that feel good on your feet and are kinder to the planet.

Every material is checked for quality and traceability before it reaches our knitting machines. That way we know exactly where each thread comes from.

Our Production

We work with small, trusted factories that pay fair wages and run on renewable energy. Our knitting process is designed to use as little yarn and water as possible.

Leftover fibres are collected and recycled into new yarn wherever we can. Less waste means brighter socks with a lighter footprint.

Our Packaging

Every pair arrives in plastic-free, fully recyclable packaging made from recycled paper. We keep our boxes small to cut down on shipping emissions.

Our labels are printed with water-based inks and our tags are made from seed paper. Even the wrapping is designed to leave a little sunshine behind.
